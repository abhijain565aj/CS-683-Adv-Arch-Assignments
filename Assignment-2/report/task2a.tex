\section{Task 2A: Instruction-Conscious L2 Replacement Policy (I-RRIP)}
\label{sec:task2a}
\subsection{RRIP baseline and policy changes}
The provided \texttt{rrip.l2c\_repl} uses \texttt{maxRRPV=3}, corresponding to four RRPV states (0--3). It initializes state to 3, inserts a miss at RRPV 2, promotes a hit to 0, chooses a line at maximum RRPV as victim, and increments RRPVs until a candidate reaches the maximum if necessary.

I-RRIP keeps the same two-bit-like 0--3 state convention and first chooses an invalid way if one exists. For a full set it preferentially evicts a non-instruction line whose RRPV is 3; if none exists, it falls back to any way with RRPV 3. If no way is yet at 3, it increments all RRPVs and repeats. The preference therefore affects victim choice only among the maximum-RRPV candidates; it does not evict an instruction line with a lower RRPV while a less-protected data line is available.

The update callback in the supplied policy accepts an \texttt{isInstruction} flag. On a hit, an instruction line is promoted to RRPV 0 and a data line to 1. On a miss, instruction lines are inserted at 1 while data lines are inserted at 2. Relative to baseline RRIP, instructions receive stronger insertion and hit protection, while data lines retain a less protected state. The policy's comments and source do not include the cache-header/call-site diff, so the precise number of integration call sites changed cannot be independently enumerated from the supplied source archive.

\subsection{Discussion}
I-RRIP lowers instruction-load MPKI on every trace: from 10.5618 to 9.8916 on Tr1 (6.34\% reduction), 17.7813 to 13.0364 on Tr2 (26.68\% reduction), and 1.1970 to 1.0221 on Tr3 (14.61\% reduction). Its IPC effect varies: Tr1 falls by 0.029\%, Tr2 rises by 1.914\%, and Tr3 rises by 0.063\%. The largest benefit in these measurements is on Tr2, where the instruction-MPKI reduction coincides with a substantial IPC increase.

The trade-off is visible in data behavior. L2 data MPKI rises from 7.0769 to 7.7172 on Tr1, 7.9645 to 9.1737 on Tr2, and 2.8424 to 2.8761 on Tr3. The aggregate L2C load-MPKI field also rises on all traces. The implementation's priority rule is consistent with the stated objective of protecting instruction lines, but the measurements show that the policy can displace useful data and does not guarantee an overall benefit. The insertion and promotion choices are simple and consistent with that objective; a more selective policy could make instruction protection conditional on demonstrated reuse or phase behavior.
